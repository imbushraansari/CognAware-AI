Distortion Intelligence Coverage

Six cognitive distortion classes are consistently mapped from model output to dashboard and result views.

Overgeneralization

Interpreting one difficult event as a permanent pattern.

Catastrophizing

Jumping quickly to the worst possible outcome.

Mind Reading

Assuming others think negatively without direct evidence.

Emotional Reasoning

Treating strong feelings as unquestionable facts.

Black & White Thinking

Viewing situations only in extremes, without middle ground.

Personalization

Taking excessive blame for events outside your control.
